Haixu Ma 马海旭

Research Scientist at Meta

I am a Research Scientist at Meta, working at the intersection of large language models, reinforcement learning, and recommendation systems. I am interested in LLM post-training, reward learning, and adaptive AI systems that improve through feedback.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Prof. Yufeng Liu and Prof. Donglin Zeng. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Statistics from Nankai University. My research spans statistical machine learning, causal inference, and personalized decision-making.
