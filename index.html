Background

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where I was advised by Professors Yufeng Liu and Donglin Zeng. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Statistics from Nankai University.

My work bridges statistical foundations and modern AI systems. I enjoy research problems that combine rigorous methodology, scalable implementation, and measurable empirical impact.

Recognition: ASA Biopharmaceutical Section Best Student Paper Award (2022); BRAIN Initiative Trainee Highlight Award Honorable Mention (2023).
